# Tylenol Dosing Calculator

A comprehensive acetaminophen (Tylenol) dosing calculator that provides safe, age and weight-based dosing recommendations for all common formulations.

## 🔗 Live Demo

[View Live Calculator](https://smbrownai.github.io/tylenol-dosing-calc/)

## 📋 Overview

This calculator helps determine appropriate Tylenol (acetaminophen) doses based on:
- Patient age (months or years)
- Patient weight (pounds or kilograms)
- Standard pediatric and adult dosing guidelines

The app provides dosing recommendations for all common Tylenol formulations including infant drops, children's liquid suspension, chewable tablets, and adult strength tablets.

## ✨ Features

- **Age-based calculations** - Input age in years or months
- **Weight-based calculations** - Input weight in pounds or kilograms
- **Multiple formulations** - Recommendations for all appropriate Tylenol products:
  - Infant Drops/Oral Suspension
  - Children's Liquid Suspension
  - Children's Chewable Tablets
  - Junior Strength Chewable Tablets
  - Regular Strength Tablets/Caplets
  - Extra Strength Tablets/Caplets
- **Safety features** - Built-in validation and maximum dose warnings
- **Responsive design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Offline capable** - No internet connection required after download
- **Privacy-focused** - No data collection or external dependencies

## ⚕️ Dosing Guidelines

This calculator follows standard acetaminophen dosing recommendations:

- **Pediatric dosing**: 10-15 mg/kg per dose
- **Frequency**: Every 4-6 hours as needed
- **Maximum**: 5 doses per 24 hours
- **Daily maximum**: Age and weight dependent (displayed in results)

### Age-Specific Formulations

- **Under 2 months**: Consult healthcare provider (calculator will not provide recommendations)
- **2 months - 3 years**: Infant drops, children's suspension
- **2-11 years**: Children's suspension, chewable tablets
- **6-11 years**: Junior strength options also available
- **12+ years**: Regular and extra strength adult formulations

## ⚠️ Important Medical Disclaimer

**This calculator provides general dosing guidelines based on standard recommendations. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice.**

- Always consult with a healthcare provider before administering medication
- Verify dosing with your pediatrician or pharmacist
- In case of emergency or suspected overdose, call Poison Control at **1-800-222-1222** or seek immediate medical attention
- Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products
- Contact a doctor if fever lasts more than 3 days or pain lasts more than 5 days
